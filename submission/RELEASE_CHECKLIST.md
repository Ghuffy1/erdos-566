- The primary submitter authorized publication and JSP submission after successful final verification.

## Public proof repository

- Publish the reviewed repository under Ghuffy1 when authorized.

- Refresh the JSP rules, the problem mapping, and the search for related submissions.
- Apply the prepared catalog-only change to an up-to-date fork of `TheJustinSunPrize/awards`.

- Let maintainers reconcile index, eligibility, and claim-status fields after review.

## Individual award applications

- After the required contribution PR merges, each contributor opens their own claim using their own account and the matching prepared draft.
- Link the actual merged contribution PR and the other contributors' claims as they become available.
- Each applicant sends the required identity-verification email from their own follow-up address to the official contact in the rules, referencing their claim and the merged PR.
- Each applicant completes the organizer's personal identity checks and written recipient confirmation.
- The organizer publishes role-specific public notices and establishes the actual 14-day review dates.
- Resolve any relevant challenges and pending reviews before the organizer confirms an award.
- Exchange payment and delivery information only through the official private process.

The external actions above are pending. The preparation does not establish an accepted claim, an active public-review period, firstness, a prize level, or a payment allocation.
